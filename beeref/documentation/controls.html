Pan Canvas

Middle Click + Drag
Alt + Left Click + Drag
Right Click + Drag in fullscreen mode on macOS
Shift + Ctrl + Mouse Wheel (pan vertically)
Shift + Mouse wheel (pan horizontally)

Zoom Canvas

Mouse Wheel (centered beneath the pointer)
Ctrl + Middle Click + Drag

Zoom in on Item

Double-click on item
Double-click again to get back to previous view

Select Items

Single Item: Left Click
Multiple Items: Ctrl + Left Click
Rubberband Selection: Left Click + Drag

Move Selected Items

Left Click + Drag

Snap Rotation

Shift or Ctrl while rotating

Move Window

Right Click + Drag when not fullscreen (macOS and Windows)
Right Click + Drag from fullscreen to restore and move (Windows)
Ctrl + Alt + Left Click + Drag
Lock / Unlock Window Position: Ctrl + Shift + L

Edit Text

Double Click on Text
Press Enter or click elsewhere to stop editing
Shift + Enter for newline

Draw on Canvas

Enter / Finish Draw Mode: Ctrl + D (Cmd + D on macOS)
Pen: D   Line: L   Rectangle: R   Ellipse: C   Eraser: E
Solid: 1   Dotted: 2   Arrow: 3
Stroke Width: [ and ]   Stroke Color: Ctrl + T
Constrain Angle or Proportions: Shift + Drag
Temporary Eraser on macOS: Hold Cmd while using the Pen
Pan While Drawing: Middle Drag or Alt + Left Drag
Edit Existing Drawing: Double-click it
Finish Drawing: Escape
Delete Selected Stroke: Delete or Backspace

Drawing controls stay anchored to the screen while the canvas moves. Choose any corner or edge-center anchor under Settings → Appearance.

Command Palette

Ctrl + Shift + P

Quick View Controls

Always on Top: Ctrl + Shift + A
Fullscreen: Ctrl + F or F11
Zoom: Mouse Wheel or Trackpad Pinch
Fit Canvas (and trim unused canvas bounds): Ctrl + Space
Focus Selection: Space
Bring to Front / Send to Back: Up / Down
