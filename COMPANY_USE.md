# Using OpenRef in an organization

This document is practical project guidance, not legal advice.

OpenRef is a standalone desktop application. Its source is licensed under GNU
GPL version 3 or later; official binaries include PyQt6 and are distributed
under GPL version 3 only. Organizations may use and modify it internally.
Using OpenRef does not place artwork, reference images, scene content, or
unrelated company software under the GPL.

## Employees and internal modifications

An organization may install OpenRef for its employees and maintain an internal
fork without publishing private modifications, provided copies remain within
that organization. The organization may own copyright in contributions made
by its employees, while the combined BeeRef-derived application remains
subject to GPLv3 when conveyed.

## Contractors and external recipients

Conservatively treat providing an OpenRef binary or private fork to an
off-site contractor, vendor, customer, or separate legal entity as conveying
the software. Give every recipient:

- the exact corresponding source for the version they receive;
- the complete GPLv3 license and attribution notices; and
- the right to copy, modify, and redistribute OpenRef under GPLv3.

Do not apply NDA or contract terms that restrict those GPL rights. Agreements
may still protect company artwork, credentials, project files, trade secrets,
and unrelated proprietary software. Do not give confidential OpenRef
modifications to an external recipient unless the organization accepts that
the recipient receives GPL rights in that version.

Organizations needing a different arrangement should have qualified counsel
review their deployment and contractor relationships.
